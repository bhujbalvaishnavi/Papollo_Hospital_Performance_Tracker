# **Papollo Hospitals – Leads Flow Dashboard**

---

## **1. Project Overview**

The **Papollo Hospitals – Leads Flow Dashboard** is a static **Power BI** solution designed to deliver comprehensive patient analytics for hospital operations. It provides a centralized view of patient flow, resource utilization, and financial performance using a structured dummy dataset.

This dashboard enables hospital administrators to monitor key operational metrics and derive actionable insights to enhance efficiency and decision-making.

---

## **2. Business Problem**

Hospitals often face challenges in:

* Monitoring patient inflow and outflow efficiently
* Managing bed occupancy across different categories
* Tracking doctor performance through patient feedback
* Comparing billing amounts with insurance coverage
* Accessing consolidated insights across multiple timeframes

The absence of a unified analytics platform leads to delayed decisions and operational inefficiencies.

---

## **3. Dashboard Objectives**

* Provide a **holistic view of hospital operations**
* Enable **real-time-like analysis** using structured visuals
* Improve **resource allocation and patient management**
* Support **data-driven decision-making** for administrators
* Simplify complex healthcare data into **intuitive visual insights**

---

## **4. Key Features & Insights**

* **Bed Occupancy Analysis**

  * Tracks utilization across **Private, General, and ICU beds**
  * Helps identify capacity constraints and optimization opportunities

* **Feedback Volume per Doctor**

  * Evaluates doctor performance based on patient feedback trends

* **Diagnosis-wise Patient Count**

  * Highlights the most common diagnoses and patient distribution

* **Billing vs Health Insurance Comparison**

  * Compares revenue generated with insurance coverage trends

* **Dynamic Date Filtering**

  * Analyze data using:

    * Admit Date
    * Discharge Date
    * Follow-Up Date

* **Interactive Slicers**

  * Filter data by:

    * Patient ID
    * Custom Date Range

* **KPI Cards**

  * Displays critical metrics such as:

    * Total Billing Amount
    * Key Dates

---

## **5. Dashboard Components**

Each visual element is designed to present specific insights:

* **KPI Cards**

  * Provide a quick snapshot of key metrics such as total billing and important dates

* **Bar/Column Charts**

  * Used for **bed occupancy** and **diagnosis distribution**

* **Line/Trend Charts**

  * Display patterns in **patient flow over time**

* **Donut/Pie Charts**

  * Represent proportional data such as **bed category usage**

* **Tables/Matrix**

  * Show detailed patient-level or doctor-level insights

* **Slicers & Filters**

  * Enable dynamic interaction and customized analysis

---

## **8. Target Audience**

* **Hospital Administrators**

---

## **9. How to Use the Dashboard**

1. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**
2. Use **slicers** to filter by Patient ID or Date Range
3. Navigate through visuals to analyze:

   * Patient distribution
   * Bed occupancy
   * Financial performance
4. Apply **date filters** (Admit, Discharge, Follow-Up) for time-based insights
5. Interpret KPI cards for quick decision-making
